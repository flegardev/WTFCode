## Engine limitations observed

- Tree-sitter completed as `partial`: one file contained syntax its worker could not fully parse, and its evidence was compacted to the fusion budget.
- Semgrep was not installed, so its adapter was isolated as `unavailable` without aborting the scan.
- ctags was not installed, so its adapter was likewise isolated as `unavailable`.
- PHP's configured memory ceiling bounded peak allocation at 128 MiB. The fused graph completed within that limit.
- Product enrichment reported `product_intelligence_limited=1`: derived relationship creation reached its explicit bounded fusion budget. Core symbol, relationship, and route limit flags remained clear.
- Disagreement counts are bounded diagnostic samples between engines, not proof that either engine is wrong.
